# School Results Portal
# Problem Statement
Many schools in Nigeria still manage students' results manually using paper records or spreadsheets. This process is slow, prone to calculation errors, can lead to lost or altered records, and often requires students to travel to school just to check their results. It also increases the workload for teachers and administrators.
Solution
The School Results Portal is a digital platform that allows schools to manage, calculate, and publish students' results online. It automatically computes grades, reduces human errors, stores records securely, and enables students to access their results quickly from any internet-enabled device.
Benefits
Reduces errors in result computation.
Speeds up result processing and publication.
Gives students quick online access to their results.
Improves record keeping and organization.
Reduces paperwork and administrative workload.
Increases transparency and accuracy in result management.
This is a practical problem faced by many schools in Nigeria
